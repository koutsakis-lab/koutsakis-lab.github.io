**Mario Cestari**  
*Ph.D. Student, Mechanical Engineering*

Mario's current research investigates the application of conventional thermal barrier coatings (TBCs) on rotating detonation engines (RDEs) to enhance thermodynamic performance. By reducing material degradation, this work aims to enable longer test runs and sustained operation under extreme combustor conditions.
