**Ioannis (Giannis) Takouridis**  
*Ph.D. Student, Mechanical Engineering*

Giannis' research focuses on the durability of thermal barrier coatings for rotating detonation engines (RDEs). He combines heat transfer modeling with fracture-based mechanics to predict fatigue damage and failure in coatings on metallic RDE combustors, and plans to validate these models through upcoming experiments. This work aims to guide materials selection for coatings that improve durability while also minimizing heat losses.
