**Amjad Samra**  
*M.S. Student, Mechanical Engineering*

Amjad's research focuses on the thermal management of rotating detonation engines (RDEs), specifically the design and testing of active water-cooling systems for an additively manufactured RDE. He designs and develops hardware for calorimetric heat flux measurements to characterize the extreme, unsteady heat loads these engines impose on the combustor walls, supporting durable, sustained RDE operation for next-generation propulsion and power applications.
