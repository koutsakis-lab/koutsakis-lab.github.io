  <div class="research-detail-top__text">
    <p>
      Ceramics and metals in a propulsion system are asked to do two things at once: insulate, and
      survive. Multilayered architectures let a designer trade between the two, but the same
      interfaces that manage heat also concentrate stress. We study that tradeoff directly, from
      coating microstructure to component-level durability.
    </p>
    <p>
      Our work in this area includes:
      <ul>
        <li><ins>Thermal barrier coatings:</ins> Performance and durability of ceramic coatings on engine components, including behavior under rotating detonation environments.</li>
        <li><ins>Fracture and delamination:</ins> Fracture-based transient thermo-mechanical analysis of coated walls, and the delamination modes that end a coating's life.</li>
        <li><ins>Coating architecture design:</ins> Optical and thermal design metrics for radiation barrier coatings and reflective insulation systems.</li>
        <li><ins>Manufacturing routes:</ins> Cold spray additive manufacturing for detonation-based rocket engine chambers, and the properties the process leaves behind.</li>
      </ul>
    </p>

    <h3>Coatings Under Detonation</h3>
    <p>
      Conventional thermal barrier coatings were qualified for steady combustion, not detonation.
      We test them in the rotating detonation environment to find out which failure modes actually
      appear and how quickly.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-detail-card">
    <div class="figure-placeholder">Figure coming soon</div>
    <h3>Fracture Mechanics</h3>
    <p>
      Transient thermo-mechanical analysis rooted in fracture mechanics predicts when a coating
      cracks or delaminates over a duty cycle, rather than only whether it insulates well on the
      first firing.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-detail-card">
    <div class="figure-placeholder">Figure coming soon</div>
    <h3>Cold Spray Manufacturing</h3>
    <p>
      Cold spray additive manufacturing offers a route to aerospace detonation-based rocket engine
      chambers. We study how the deposited material behaves thermally and mechanically once it is
      in service.
    </p>
