The group will present four talks and two posters at the [2026 New Mexico Research Symposium](https://www.nmepscor.org/engagement/NM-Research-Symposium) (NMRS) on Saturday, November 7, at the UNM Student Union Building. Congratulations to all of the presenters! Abstracts: [oral](https://www.nmepscor.org/sites/default/files/nmrs/2026/2026_NMRS_Oral_Abstracts.pdf) and [poster](https://www.nmepscor.org/sites/default/files/nmrs/2026/2026_NMRS_Poster_Abstracts.pdf).

**Oral Presentations**

- Carldon Pascual will present *Modeling the Behavior of Thermal Radiation in Translucent Ceramic Barrier Coatings* (Session C: New Mexico Space Grant Fellows, Acoma A/B). Authors: C. Pascual, M. Presby (NASA), J. Eldridge (NASA), B. Harder (NASA), G. Koutsakis.
- Brendan Canales will present *Control System Design for a Rotating Detonation Engine* (Session C: New Mexico Space Grant Fellows, Acoma A/B).
- Dimitrios Vrocharis will present *Canonical Performance Parameters for Rotating Detonation Engines* (Session G: Engineering & Materials Science, Lobo B). Authors: D. Vrocharis, M. Cestari, B. Canales, M. Wolbrecht, K. Johnson (National Energy Technology Laboratory), E. Unruh (Aerojet Rocketdyne).
- Amjad Samra will present *Sustained Heat Flux Measurements in an Air Breathing Rotating Detonation Engine* (Session G: Engineering & Materials Science, Lobo B). Authors: A. Samra, D. Terzis (Aristotle University of Thessaloniki), H. Holliday (NTU), M. Wolbrecht, D. Vrocharis, M. Cestari, B. Canales, P. Panagiotou (Aristotle University of Thessaloniki), G. Koutsakis.

**Poster Presentations**

- Mario Cestari will present *HADES, the New Mexico Rotating Detonation Engine Materials Testbed Facility* (Poster 18B). Authors: M. Cestari, D. Vrocharis, M. Wolbrecht, B. Canales, L. Bermudez, K. Reyes-Contreras, A. Ramirez De Jesus, J. Williams, E. Unruh (Aerojet Rocketdyne), J. Stout (Aerojet Rocketdyne), G. Koutsakis.
- Carldon Pascual will present *Modeling the Behavior of Thermal Radiation in Translucent Ceramic Barrier Coatings* (Poster 21A). Authors: C. Pascual, M. Presby (NASA), J. Eldridge (NASA), B. Harder (NASA), G. Koutsakis.
