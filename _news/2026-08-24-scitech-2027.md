Four papers from the group were accepted to [AIAA SciTech 2027](https://www.aiaa.org/SciTech): *Canonical Performance Parameters for Rotating Detonation Engine Materials* (Vrocharis et al.), *Conventional Thermal Barrier Coatings Tested Under Rotating Detonation Environments* (Cestari et al.), *Calorimetric Heat Flux Measurements in a Hydrogen–Air Rotating Detonation Engine* (Samra et al.), and *Semi-transparent Ceramic System Radiation Modeling Validation Against Experimental Data* (Pascual et al.).
