Thermodynamic equilibrium, thermodynamic properties and equations of state. First and second laws of thermodynamics and their applications to engineering systems. Availability and irreversibility and their application to second law analysis. Three credit hours. Prerequisites: CHEM 1215, CHEM 1215L, MATH 2531, and PHYS 1320. Taught Spring 2024.
