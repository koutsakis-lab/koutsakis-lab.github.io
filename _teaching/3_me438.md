---
The course introduces students to the fundamentals of space propulsion concepts, analysis, and design, and provides the necessary background of the physical processes that form the basis of different propulsion methods. Three credit hours. Prerequisites: ME 301 and ME 317L. Offered to both undergraduate (438) and graduate (538) students. Taught Fall 2024, Fall 2025, and Fall 2026.
