Independent research experience in the Koutsakis Lab, carried out under the direct supervision of Prof. Koutsakis on an active project. Per the UNM catalog: "A project of an original nature carried out under faculty supervision" (451, undergraduate); "Individual research into an area proposed by the student and conducted under the direction of a faculty member" ([551](https://catalog.unm.edu/catalogs/2022-2023/courses/ME/551-552.html), graduate). One to three credit hours, to a maximum of six. Prerequisites: admission to the B.S.M.E. program and senior standing (451), or consent of instructor (551). For undergraduates, ME 451/452 is also the standard first step toward Mechanical Engineering Departmental Honors: per the [ME Undergraduate Handbook](https://me.unm.edu/students/undergraduate/me_undergraduate_handbook_fall2025_10-15-2025.pdf#page=13) (Section 16, "Departmental Honors," pp. 13–14), one or more semesters of ME 451/452 provide the individual-study research on which an [ME 463 Undergraduate Honors Thesis](https://catalog.unm.edu/catalogs/2022-2023/courses/ME/463.html) is later based. Taught Fall.
