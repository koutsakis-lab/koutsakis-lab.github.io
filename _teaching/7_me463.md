The capstone course for Mechanical Engineering Departmental Honors, completed under Prof. Koutsakis's supervision by students who have finished one or more semesters of ME 451/452 in the lab. Per the UNM catalog: "Independent project of an original nature carried out under faculty supervision, in partial fulfillment of Departmental Honors designation." Three credit hours. Restriction: admission to the School of Engineering and instructor permission required. Per the [ME Undergraduate Handbook](https://me.unm.edu/students/undergraduate/me_undergraduate_handbook_fall2025_10-15-2025.pdf#page=13) (Section 16, "Departmental Honors," pp. 13–14), the honors thesis is "a scholarly work based upon the research performed in the individual study course(s)" — i.e., the ME 451/452 project the student already completed.
