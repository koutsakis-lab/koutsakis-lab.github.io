---
title: "ME 461/561: Fire Dynamics and Science"
---
An introduction to the science of fire and its dynamics, from ignition and flame spread through compartment fires and fire resistance. The course is team-taught: Prof. Koutsakis coordinates the course and serves as instructor of record, with co-instructors from UNM Mechanical Engineering, UNM Civil, Construction and Environmental Engineering, Sandia National Laboratories, Los Alamos National Laboratory, and the CNM Fire Academy leading sessions in their areas of expertise. Coursework combines problem sets drawn from Drysdale's *An Introduction to Fire Dynamics* with a term project applying fire-dynamics analysis to a wildland–urban interface, compartment-fire, or fire-safety problem. Three credit hours. Prerequisites: ME 301 and ME 320L, or consent of instructor. Taught Fall 2026.
