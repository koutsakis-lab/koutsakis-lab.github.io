Principles and engineering applications of heat transfer by conduction, convection and radiation. Laboratory experiments and demonstrations of fundamental heat transfer concepts. Four credit hours. Prerequisites: ME 301, ME 317L, and MATH 316. Taught Spring 2025 and Spring 2026.
